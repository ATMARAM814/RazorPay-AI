Compliance Rules & Stopping Mechanics

NPCI UPI Ceiling (4 Attempts Max)

Under NPCI UPI recurring guidelines, any mandate reaching 4 total attempts (1 initial + 3 retries) is automatically halted to prevent system suspension.

RBI Retry Window Spacing

Retries are spaced across RBI-compliant windows (~24h $\rightarrow$ 72h $\rightarrow$ 168h). Rapid-fire retries are strictly blocked.

Revocation Restraint

If a customer revokes their mandate (`mandate_revoked`), the system takes `no_action_respect_revoke` immediately, stopping un-compliant charges.
